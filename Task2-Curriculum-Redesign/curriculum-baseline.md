# Task 2 — 2030 CSE Curriculum Redesign

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the baseline for reviewing the existing B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) programme and proposing a curriculum aligned with 2030 industry requirements.

The career focus is:
- Full-Stack Software Engineer
- AI/ML Engineer

## 2. Official Curriculum Source

- Institution: ICFAI Tech School, Hyderabad
- Programme: Regular B.Tech — Computer Science & Engineering
- Official programme structure: https://www.ifheindia.org/icfai-tech-school-hyderabad/icfaitech-btech-program-structure
- Source access date: 22 October 2026
- Source type: Official institutional webpage

The final report must distinguish the published curriculum from proposed changes. The published page must be checked again before submission, and the applicable curriculum version for the student's batch must be confirmed where possible.

## 4. Important Verification Note

The published Semester VII/VIII table appears ambiguous regarding the listed Internship/Research and Thesis & Seminar entries and their credits. Do not assume that every listed entry is an additional mandatory 15-credit requirement in each semester. Verify the applicable structure before calculating total programme credits.

## 5. Planned Audit Method

Each course will be reviewed against:
1. Its contribution to full-stack software engineering.
2. Its contribution to AI/ML engineering.
3. The skills requested in three real employer job descriptions.
4. Potential duplication, outdated coverage, or missing practical skills.
5. Opportunities for AI-assisted exercises with human verification.
6. Proposed changes and their credit impact.

## 6. Evidence and Integrity Rules

- Use official institutional sources for the curriculum.
- Use real employer job descriptions and record their URLs and access dates.
- Label published facts, estimates, assumptions and proposals separately.
- Do not invent course credits, job requirements or evidence of institutional practices.
- Calculate the redesign percentage only after the verified programme-credit denominator is established.
